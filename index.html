Dealer Signature Analysis

Every dealer develops a "signature"—a repeatable rhythm in spin behavior and travel distance. By studying travel behavior, you react to identifiable structures.

Travel Data Insights

  • Big Travel: High volatility, large jumps, frequent direction switches.
  • Small Travel: Phase keeping, consistent rhythm, higher "near hit" probability.
IDEAL ENVIRONMENT:
Stable small travel with consistent direction.

Pattern Recognition

Prepare to enter after a Loss appears.

Trend: Hot Streak

W W W L W W

Frequent wins with rare losses indicate a trending table. Ride while structure holds.

TOP Numbers (Racetrack)

TOP OVER

Travel line is ABOVE the orange line.

TOP UNDER

Travel line is BELOW the orange line.
Format: 9n4 (Number 9 with 4 neighbors)

🚫 Tables to Avoid

  • Inconsistent travel distance (Random jumps)
  • Clustered losses without any rhythm
  • Dealer constantly changing spin strength

RULE: No structure = No edge.

PRO TIP: Never chase losses. Wait for a structured opportunity, then execute with discipline. Exit at the first sign of pattern weakening.
